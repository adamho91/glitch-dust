2 · Container fill
Cover / Contain / Stretch make the embed fill 100% of its parent — in Webflow set the parent element to position: relative with a real height. Adapt rebuilds the pattern at the container's exact size (no cropping, re-generates on resize). Natural behaves like the standard embed (full width, aspect-ratio height).
3 · Playback & interaction
1.00×
Slows or speeds the whole animation — loop, drift, bursts, glitch — without changing its character. 0.25× = quarter speed (calm), 2× = double.
160px
Nodes within the proximity radius of the user's mouse, trackpad, or thumb stop moving and hold their current look; everything outside keeps animating. Radius is in source-canvas pixels. Try it in the preview.
4 · Palette media (masks)
Load a pattern to see its palette.
Reveals an image through every node of that palette color — the embed version of Glitch Dust's Mask image / video per palette color. Uploads are inlined into the embed and auto-compressed until the snippet fits Webflow's 50k limit (the status line reports what happened); a hosted asset URL (Webflow asset / CDN) keeps full quality and the smallest embed. Images work everywhere; GIFs may show a single frame; video isn't supported in embeds.

Or drag the dashed box's corner in the preview to test any container shape. Padding simulates your Webflow container's padding; Backdrop paints the preview window behind the embed to match your site's background (preview only, never exported).
5 · Export
Webflow: Add → Embed → paste. The snippet is one self-contained HTML block (minified runtime inlined, no external files) and the status line reports its character count against Webflow's 50,000 limit.
